An interview with Chris Gales, Memphis singer-songwriter, guitarist, and streamer on Happs. Memphis guitarist, singer, songwriter Chris Gales has been performing for audiences from all over on world famous Beale Street for six years and began streaming his performances live four years ago. We talked to Chris about his musical journey, growing his fanbase on Happs , and his tips for other livestreaming musicians.
